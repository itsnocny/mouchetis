      What mouchetis does

      It finds the person, rebuilds the body as dots whose density follows the light of the photo, and replaces the face with dots that are computed only from the pixels around it. No original pixel is kept.

      Everything runs in your browser. The photo is not sent anywhere. After the models are loaded you can turn off your connection and it still works.

      After each render the page runs the face detector again on the result. If it still finds a face where the face was erased, it tells you.

      What it does not hide

      The body stays recognizable by its outline and posture. Tattoos, moles, jewelry and scars larger than the dots stay visible.

      In a mirror photo the face can appear twice, and a blurry or side-on reflection can be missed. If a phone or a hand covers the face, the erased area is estimated from the posture. Check it with More, Show, Mask placement.

      A photo already posted somewhere else can still be found, whatever you do to it.
